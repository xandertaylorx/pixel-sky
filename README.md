# Pixel Sky

An ambient pixel-art clock. The sun and moon sit where they really are for your location, the weather matches what's outside, and the scene changes with where you are (London, Manchester, Paris, Tokyo and more, or a lake and mountains elsewhere).

**Live:** https://xandertaylorx.github.io/pixel-sky/

Works well as a second-monitor or tablet display. Press `F` for fullscreen, `S` for settings.

Weather by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com). Sun and moon positions are computed locally.
